The game logic works (bot clears the level, 241 tests pass) but the picture is wrong. Reviewer findings:
1. **CANVAS OFF-SCREEN (critical).** `Game.ts` (around line 116) does `document.body.appendChild(canvas)` AFTER the `#app` div (which is 100vh tall), so the canvas is laid out at y = 768, below the viewport, and `body{overflow:hidden}` hides it: the screen is black except the HUD. Fix: make the canvas `position: fixed; inset: 0; width: 100vw; height: 100vh; z-index: 0; display:block` (in styles.css or on the element) and make sure HUD/screens overlays have a higher z-index. Also make `Renderer.setSize` use `window.innerWidth/innerHeight` on `resize`.
2. **OVEREXPOSED / WASHED OUT.** The whole picture is a white-orange haze. Fix in `render/Renderer.ts` and the materials: `toneMappingExposure` 0.85; UnrealBloomPass strength 0.55, radius 0.5, **threshold 0.75** (so only truly bright things bloom); lower the floor emissive intensity to ~0.55 (it is currently ~1.6 and blooms the whole floor); lane lines/rails/obstacle emissives may stay bright (intensity 1.5–2.5) so they glow against a DARK floor and dark background. Target look: near-black scene with thin glowing neon lines (orange circuit traces, cyan lane lines, magenta walls), like the reference.
