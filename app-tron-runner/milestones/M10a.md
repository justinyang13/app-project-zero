## DESIGN (decided; do not redesign, do not spend time planning — write `bot.ts` in your FIRST edit, then iterate with the simulation)
`client/src/logic/bot.ts` exports `createBot()` and `botActions(state, level, dist, simTime): PlayerActions` (pure; use the same action type that `stepPlayer` takes; press an action only on the tick it should fire — edge-triggered — and rate-limit lane presses to one per 0.25 s via a field on the bot object).
Per tick:
1. Look ahead `L = speed * 1.3` metres (use `level.obstaclesInRange(dist, dist+L)`, `bitsInRange`).
2. Lane choice (floor): for each lane in {-1,0,1} cost = 1000 if a `block` or an unavoidable hazard is in that lane within L, else 3 × (number of barrier/beam/gap/drone/laser within L in that lane) + 0.8 × |lane − currentLane| − 0.2 × (bits in that lane). Move one lane toward the cheapest lane (press left/right) if it is cheaper than staying by > 1.
3. In the current lane, take the nearest hazard ahead at distance d with speed v: barrier or gap → press jump when d <= v*0.28; beam or laser (when it will be OFF on arrival) → press slide when d <= v*0.22; drone → pick the lane/timing where `droneX(time at arrival)` is > 1.6 m from the lane x, otherwise change lane; laser ON on arrival → wait (do nothing) / change lane; rival → change lane; boss telegraphed lane → leave that lane before the bolt arrives; longGap → be in lane ±1 on the side of its wall strip ≥ 14 m before the gap and press the direction toward the wall at ≥ 10 m before it, stay on wall until past the gap, then press away; wallBlock on the wall → press jump (high row) or slide (low row) to take the opposite row.
4. Always stay in the same cycle/runner mode rules (no slide / wall in cycle mode).
